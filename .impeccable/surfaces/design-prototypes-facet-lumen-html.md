# Lumen six-journey extension

Mode: Operate. The project selected Lumen on 13 September 2026.
This is an extension of that rendered and selected direction, not a new concept tournament.

THESIS: Ordinary Android tasks should feel familiar, clear, beautiful, and responsive.
OWN-WORLD: Preserve Lumen's porcelain/cobalt roles, platform typography, visible search,
app grid, and grouped Settings. Let task content determine emphasis rather than repeating hero panels.
STORY: Set up, find an app, change scoped privacy, recover notifications, request a charge
limit, and safely follow update stages with cancellation and truthful outcomes.
FIRST VIEWPORT: One interactive phone, six entry points, and review controls outside the OS.
FORM: Extend the user-selected Lumen candidate from seed c2988422. The explicit selection
supersedes additional direction/comp approval rounds. No new raster asset is needed.
FINISH: Preserve the baseline/comparison, inspect rendered flows and stressed layouts,
record the selected tokens/motion and distinguish browser results from native acceptance.
